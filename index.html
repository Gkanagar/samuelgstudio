FAQ

        
Questions, answered.

      

      

        

          How much does a card cost?
          
Each card is £4.50, and standard UK delivery is free. Express 1st Class delivery is £1 extra per order.

        

        

          How long does it take?
          
Please allow at least 5 days: a couple of days to draw your card, then 2–3 working days by Royal Mail 2nd Class (or usually next working day with Express).

        

        

          Can you send it straight to the recipient?
          
Yes. Tick "Send directly to the recipient" and enter their address. Tell us what to write inside in the details box.

        

        

          Can I ask for something specific?
          
Of course. Use the details box to tell us colours, characters, their favourite team, or the exact words you'd like inside.

        

        

          What if something's wrong with my card?
          
If it arrives damaged, has a mistake we made, or doesn't arrive, we'll redraw it or refund you in full. Because every card is personalised, we can't take returns for a change of mind. See Returns & refunds.

        

        

          Do you deliver outside the UK?
          
Not yet. We currently deliver within the UK only.
