Delivery

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: we deliver anywhere in the UK. Standard delivery is free; Express is £1 per order. Please order at least 5 days before you need your card.

Where we deliver

We currently deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom only. We can send your card to you, or directly to the person it's for.

Delivery options and prices

  • Standard UK delivery: free. Royal Mail 2nd Class, usually 2–3 working days after posting.
  • Express UK delivery: £1.00 per order. Royal Mail 1st Class, usually the next working day after posting.

Delivery times are Royal Mail's estimates and aren't guaranteed. Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding UK bank holidays.

How long it takes

Every card is drawn by hand after you order, which usually takes 1–2 days. We then post it. Please allow at least 5 days between ordering and the date you need it. Around busy times (Christmas, Valentine's Day, Mother's and Father's Day) please allow a little longer.

When you order, you tell us the date you need the card by. If we can't make that date, we'll email you before taking payment, or offer a full refund.

Packaging

Cards are sent in a quality envelope, with card backing for larger orders so they arrive flat and undamaged.

If your card hasn't arrived

Royal Mail occasionally delays post. If your card hasn't arrived within 10 working days of posting, email us at hello@samuelgstudio.co.uk and we'll draw and send a replacement free of charge, or give you a full refund.

Wrong address

Please check the delivery address carefully before ordering. If a card can't be delivered because the address given was incorrect or incomplete, we can send a replacement, but we may need to charge for it.
